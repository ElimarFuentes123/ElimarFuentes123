# EP01 — phone battery (hook = relatable complaint in the first sentence)
Why does my phone battery lie to me like we're in a toxic relationship?
It says twenty percent. I blink. It's at three.
Three!
Where did seventeen percent go? Did it move out?
And the second I actually need it, it starts acting dramatic.
I open the camera to record something iconic, and it goes, "low battery, would you like to turn on low power mode?"
No. I would like you to do your job.
Then I plug it in for five minutes, and it's at six percent, like it did me a favor.
Six. I could have charged a potato faster.
And don't even get me started on the chargers.
Every charger in my house has a personality.
One only works if I hold it at a perfect forty five degree angle.
One only works when I'm not looking at it.
And one just charges the vibes.
So I finally get my phone to a hundred, I'm feeling powerful, I leave the house like the main character.
Ten minutes later? Eighty one percent.
I didn't even touch it, bro.
I opened one app. One.
And now my phone is hot enough to cook breakfast on.
At this point I'm not charging my phone. I'm babysitting it.
