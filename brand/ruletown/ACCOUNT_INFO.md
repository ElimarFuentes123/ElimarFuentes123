## Steps to create the channel (≈5 minutes, on your phone or PC)
1. Sign in to YouTube with your Google account (a new Gmail just for the channel is easier to manage).
2. Profile picture (top right) → **Create a channel**. On PC: Settings → **Create a new channel** if you want it separate from your personal name.
3. Enter the name **RULE TOWN** and the handle **@ruletownstories** → **Create channel**.
4. **Customize channel** → upload `profile_picture.png` and `youtube_banner.png` → paste the description above.
5. Settings → Channel → add keywords, country, and the audience answer.
6. Verify your phone number (youtube.com/verify). This unlocks custom thumbnails, longer videos and live streaming.
7. Upload `renders/rain-lies.mp4` with **Create → Upload video**. It's vertical and under 3 minutes, so it becomes a **Short** automatically. Paste the title and description, then publish.

## Posting rhythm
- Shorts do best with consistency: 2–3 stories per week, posted at the same time each day (for example 7 PM PH time).
- Reply to comments in the first hour, and pin a "next rule?" comment on every video. Fan rules give you the next episode ideas.
