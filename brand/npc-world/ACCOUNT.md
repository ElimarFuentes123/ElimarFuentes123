## Account setup steps
1. Sign in to YouTube with the Google account you want to use (a new Gmail keeps it separate from your other channels).
2. Profile icon → **Create a channel** (or **Switch account → Create a channel** if you already have one; this makes a Brand Account, which lets you add managers later).
3. Enter name **NPC Nico** and handle **@NPCNico**, upload `profile_picture.png`.
4. YouTube Studio → **Customization → Branding**: banner and watermark.
5. **Customization → Basic info**: description, links (TikTok, Instagram), contact email.
6. **Settings**: the category, country, audience, keywords and upload defaults above.
7. Verify the phone number (Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility) so you can upload videos longer than 15 minutes and add custom thumbnails.
