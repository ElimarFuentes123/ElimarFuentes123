In this world, every time the player loads a save, everything resets.
Crashed cars fix themselves. Broken windows grow back.
Even the guy who got hit by a bus gets up and buys a hot dog.
And nobody remembers anything.
Except me.
I'm Nico. I'm an NPC. My only job is to walk from the bus stop to the corner. Forever.
But I remember everything.
My dad's car has been stolen 4,000 times.
The player has crashed into my bus stop 300 times.
So I started leaving notes for myself, behind the dumpster. Day 1. Day 2. Day 900.
Then one night, at 2:13 AM, something was different.
The player didn't steal a car. He didn't run. He just stood there, staring at me.
Then a truck lost control.
Game over. For both of us.
Reload.
He came back earlier. The truck hit us again. Reload. Again. 47 times.
On try 48, he grabbed my arm and pulled me out of the way.
Then I saw it. Behind the dumpster, next to my notes, in his handwriting.
Day 1. Day 2. Day 900. Save Nico.
In this world, nobody remembers when the player reloads. Except me. And now, him.
