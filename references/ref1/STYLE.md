| 23.4–23.9s | Outro | Black, creator tag "SHAKIL". |

## Recipe to apply
- Cuts locked to beats (detected with `hyperframes beats`), double-time cuts in the intro.
- Intro in B&W high contrast, main section colored with dark teal/orange night grade.
- Effects: white flash, glitch split, chromatic aberration, light leak, slight shake on hits.
- Kinetic typography: car name word by word on beats, plus a stat (top speed) and a short quote.
